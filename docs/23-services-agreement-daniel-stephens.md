## 1. Background

Client operates a domestic violence shelter serving Indiana survivors and
their families. Contractor has performed professional Salesforce
administration, systems integration, data migration, and grant-management
services for Client over the course of one calendar quarter. The Parties
enter into this Agreement to memorialize the scope of those services and
the compensation due for them.

## 2. Scope of Services

Contractor has provided, and shall be compensated for, the following
professional services (collectively, the "Services") covering **one (1)
quarter** of engagement (the "Service Period," from
**__________________, 2026** to **__________________, 2026**):

### 2.1 Salesforce Platform Management
- Administration and configuration of Client's Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud
  org, including objects, fields, page layouts, permission sets, sharing
  rules, validation rules, and Lightning apps.
- Ongoing maintenance, sandbox-first change management, and a documented
  production-cutover process.
- Knowledge-transfer documentation and a staff training walkthrough.

### 2.2 Integration
- Design and delivery of the website-to-CRM integration connecting Client's
  public intake forms to Salesforce, so survivor submissions create records
  in the org in near real time, with no protected information transmitted by
  email or left at rest in intermediate files.

### 2.3 Migration of Paper / File-Based Records
- Review of Client's existing paper and file-based workflows and migration
  of those workflows into structured Salesforce objects (including financial
  counseling, self-sufficiency matrix, referral, shelter-stay, and
  mandatory-reporting records), replacing manual paper and spreadsheet
  processes with auditable digital records.

### 2.4 Grant Management, Research, and Application
- Build-out of grant-lifecycle tracking (researching → application →
  submitted → awarded → reporting → closed), including application
  deadlines and reporting due dates, to support VOCA / VAWA / FVPSA and
  similar grant requirements.
- Grant research and application support associated with the above.

## 3. Compensation

In full consideration for the Services, Client shall pay Contractor a fixed
fee of **two thousand five hundred U.S. dollars (US $2,500.00)**.

This is a flat fee for the entire Service Period and is not an hourly or
recurring charge. No additional amounts (overtime, expenses, taxes withheld,
or otherwise) are owed under this Agreement unless separately agreed in
writing by both Parties.

## 5. Independent Contractor Status

Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, agent,
or joint venturer of Client. Contractor is solely responsible for all
federal, state, and local taxes on amounts paid under this Agreement. Client
will not withhold taxes and may issue an IRS Form 1099 if required by law.
Contractor is not entitled to any employee benefits.

## 6. Work Product and Ownership

All configurations, metadata, documentation, and other deliverables created
by Contractor specifically for Client under this Agreement ("Work Product")
are the property of Client upon full payment. Contractor assigns to Client
all right, title, and interest in such Work Product. Contractor retains the
right to reuse general skills, methods, and know-how.

## 7. Confidentiality

Contractor acknowledges that, in performing the Services, Contractor may be
exposed to highly sensitive information, including information protected
under the Violence Against Women Act (VAWA) and Client's confidentiality
obligations to survivors. Contractor shall:

- hold all such information in strict confidence and use it solely to
  perform the Services;
- not disclose any survivor or client information to any third party; and
- continue these obligations indefinitely after this Agreement ends.

Contractor confirms that no real survivor or client data was retained,
exported, or used outside Client's systems in performing the Services.

## 8. Warranties

Contractor warrants that the Services were performed in a professional and
workmanlike manner consistent with applicable industry standards.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Except for breach of Section 7 (Confidentiality), neither Party's aggregate
liability under this Agreement shall exceed the total fees paid hereunder
(US $2,500.00). Neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental, or
consequential damages.

## 10. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Indiana, without
regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

## 11. Entire Agreement

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its
subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions. It may be amended only
by a writing signed by both Parties. If any provision is held unenforceable,
the remaining provisions remain in effect.
